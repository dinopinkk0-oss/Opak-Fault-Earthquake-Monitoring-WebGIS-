About the Opak Fault

Learn about the active fault that extends across the Yogyakarta region.

Source: PuSGeN 2024

The Opak Fault is an active fault in the Special Region of Yogyakarta. It follows the Opak River from Prambanan in the north to the southern coast near Parangtritis, Bantul. In geological and seismic studies, the fault is recognized as an active structure that plays an important role in the region's tectonic setting and earthquake hazard.

According to the PuSGeN 2024 publication, the Opak Fault is an active fault whose dominant movement is strike-slip, with possible shallow deformation that can trigger earthquakes in densely populated areas. This makes the fault a key focus for disaster risk reduction, as it crosses areas with dense settlements, infrastructure, and economic activity.

The magnitude 6.3 Yogyakarta earthquake on May 27, 2006, was a significant event that strengthened understanding of the relationship between Opak Fault activity and the damage in Bantul and surrounding areas. It also demonstrated that shaking from this fault may affect not only nearby areas but also communities farther along its path.

Earthquake Preparedness

How to prepare for earthquakes in the Opak Fault hazard area.

Before an Earthquake

  • Identify evacuation routes and assembly points near your home or workplace.
  • Prepare an emergency kit with essential supplies.
  • Make sure buildings follow earthquake-resistant construction standards.
  • Take part in preparedness training or drills regularly.

During an Earthquake

  • Drop, cover, and hold on under a sturdy table.
  • Stay away from glass, shelves, and objects that could fall.
  • If outdoors, move away from buildings and power lines.
  • Stay calm. Do not run or use elevators.

After an Earthquake

  • Check yourself and those around you for injuries.
  • Be alert for aftershocks.
  • Turn off electricity and gas if you suspect a leak.
  • Follow official updates from BMKG and relevant authorities.

Emergency Kit Essentials

Drinking water, non-perishable food, personal medication, a flashlight, a radio, important documents, and some cash. Keep the kit somewhere easy to reach and check its contents regularly.
